# Independence OS — website rebuild

## Context
- Client site: https://independenceos.ai — healthcare AI company that owns/operates clinics (TX + CA) and builds the AI OS that runs them. Pre-launch, v1. Houston + Bangalore.
- `Research Dossier.dc.html` = source of truth for site content, anatomy, copy system, and rebuild concepts. Read it before designing.

## Mission
Rebuild as a premium scroll-driven website with WebGL elements, at awwwards-level craft.

## Working rules
- Original design: draw on the live site's content, voice, and narrative devices — never clone its visuals.
- Keep site copy verbatim unless asked to rewrite. Voice: quiet, declarative, sentence case with periods, italic pivot words, "one" is the load-bearing word.
- Core storytelling devices to preserve: the 10 live product vignettes, the Maria Reyes patient thread, clinic-at-the-center ecosystem, "proven in our own clinics first" refrain.
- Light color scheme, clinical-editorial. US healthcare audience (physicians, practice owners, builders, investors).
- WebGL is progressive enhancement: DOM-first content for SEO, prefers-reduced-motion fallback, mobile fallbacks, 60fps budget.
- Ask before inventing new sections or copy.
